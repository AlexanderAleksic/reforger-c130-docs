This page covers the approach, touchdown and rollout.

## Approach checklist

The checklist display switches to **APPROACH / LANDING** when you lower the gear or descend below 900 m above the ground. It wants:

- gear **down**,
- flaps **set** (down),
- GPWS on, lights on,
- cargo secured,
- autopilot **off** before touchdown.

The hint line reads "GEAR NOT DOWN", "DISENGAGE AUTOPILOT BEFORE TOUCHDOWN" or "FLAPS FOR LANDING" until you are configured. Then it reads "STABLE - FLY IT DOWN".

## Procedure

<Steps>

1. **Slow down and descend.** Reduce throttle with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>. If you used the autopilot, disengage it now (**Disengage autopilot**, or move the stick).

2. **Gear down.** Press <kbd>N</kbd> (or **Lower landing gear**). It takes about 3 seconds to extend.

3. **Flaps down.** Press <kbd>F</kbd> (or **Lower flaps**). They extend over 2 seconds and add lift and drag.

4. **Lights.** Hold <kbd>L</kbd> for the landing lights (tap <kbd>L</kbd> toggles the taxi lights separately).

5. **Fly the approach** at roughly **210-230 km/h (115-125 kt)** with flaps down. Control the descent rate with throttle and pitch.

6. **Touch down gently** at roughly **185-200 km/h (100-110 kt)**, main wheels first, with the nose slightly up.

7. **Throttle to idle** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>), then engage **thrust reverse** (<kbd>U</kbd>) and add throttle (<kbd>Shift</kbd>) to slow down.

8. **Brake** by holding <kbd>Space</kbd>.

9. **At walking pace**, throttle to idle and turn thrust reverse **off** (<kbd>U</kbd>). Otherwise the aircraft starts rolling backwards.

10. **Taxi clear** and continue with [Shutdown and parking](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/shutdown/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
The speeds above are estimates worked out from the aircraft's wing and weight values. At full fuel and no cargo, the wings stall at around 160 km/h with flaps down and about 170 km/h with flaps up. A heavier aircraft stalls faster and needs more speed. Light aircraft can fly slower.
</Aside>

## Touchdown limits

The gear checks how hard you land:

| Sink rate at touchdown | Result |
|---|---|
| Below 4 m/s (about 790 ft/min) | Normal landing. |
| 4 m/s or more | Hard landing: at least one tyre blows. A blown tyre sags, grips poorly, brakes badly and pulls the aircraft toward that side. |
| 7 m/s or more (about 1,380 ft/min) | Gear collapse: the aircraft drops onto its belly and loses a quarter of its hull health. |

A **GEAR FAULT** warning appears on the checklist and warning panel after either result. Repairing the aircraft fully clears the damage. See [Fire and damage](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/fire-and-damage/).

<Aside type="danger">
Landing with the gear up drops the aircraft onto its belly. The approach checklist and the gear warning are there to catch it.
</Aside>

## Thrust reverse

Thrust reverse turns the propeller thrust backwards:

- Reverse thrust is **40% of forward thrust at your current throttle**. At idle it does nothing. Engage it, then add throttle.
- It stays on until you press <kbd>U</kbd> again (or use **Thrust reverse: OFF** on the throttle quadrant). A reverse icon shows on the vehicle HUD while it is engaged.
- It is not blocked in the air. Only engage it once you are on the ground.
- On the ground you can use it to back up slowly.

## Braking and the parking brake

- **Wheel brakes** (hold <kbd>Space</kbd>) only work with weight on the wheels. Full braking stops the aircraft firmly but not instantly. Use reverse thrust first at high speed.
- **Parking brake**: tap <kbd>Space</kbd> or use **Parking brake: RELEASED - set** on the gear panel. It only sets below about 11 km/h ("Too fast - slow to a walk first"). Once set it holds the aircraft completely still.
