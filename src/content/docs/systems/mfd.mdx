This page covers the four multi-function displays (MFDs) across the instrument panel: what each screen shows, how to change pages, how to zoom in on a screen so you can read it, and the full in-cockpit checklist.

## The four screens

The screens sit in a row across the main panel. The two outer screens are flight displays. The two centre screens each hold several pages that you can switch between.

| Screen | Pages | Opens on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer left | Flight display | Flight display |
| Centre left | **Engine**, **Weight**, **Checklist**, **Manifest** | Engine |
| Centre right | **Map** (with the release computer and the route), **Radar**, **Fuel**, **Systems**, **STARS**, **NAV** | Map |
| Outer right | Flight display | Flight display |

All pages refresh several times a second. They read live data from the aircraft, so a number that moves means something in the aircraft has actually changed.

Colours are the same on every page:

- **Green**: normal, or item done.
- **Amber**: needs attention, or in progress.
- **Red**: warning, act now.
- **Dim green**: off or not in use.

## Using the screens

Only the pilot and co-pilot can use the screens. Each screen has an interaction point on its bezel. Look at it while seated to get the screen's actions in the action menu.

| Action | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page switch | Centre screens | Steps to the next page on that screen. |
| Power | All screens | Turns that screen on or off. |
| **Focus screen** | Centre screens | Zooms your view onto the screen (see below). |
| Map zoom | Centre right, Map page only | Changes the map zoom level. |
| **Release scale** | Centre right, Map page only | Sets the scale of the release-computer marker. |
| **Release cargo** | Centre right | Drops the next load. See [Airdrop](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/airdrop/). |
| **Radar range: N KM** | Centre right, Radar page only | Changes the radar range. See [Radar](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/radar/). |
| **Flight plan: ...**, **Approach runway: ...** | Centre right, Map and NAV pages | Build the flight plan and choose the approach runway. See [Flight plan and approach](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/navigation/). |

The zoom, scale and range actions only appear while their page is on screen, so the action list stays short on the other pages.

### Focus screen

From the seat, the centre screens are small and seen at an angle. **Focus screen** moves your camera square in front of the screen with a narrow field of view, so even the small text on the checklist or fuel page is easy to read.

<Steps>

1. Seated as pilot or co-pilot, look at the centre screen you want to read.
2. Select **Focus screen** from the action menu.
3. Read the page. You keep full control of the aircraft while the view is up.
4. Press the interact key again (<kbd>F</kbd> on keyboard and mouse, <kbd>X</kbd> on gamepad) to return to the normal cockpit view.

</Steps>

The focus view also closes on its own if you open a menu, open the Game Master editor, leave your seat or die.

<Aside type="tip">
Focus the screen before you start working through the checklist or fuel panel. It turns a squint into a quick glance.
</Aside>

## Flight displays (outer screens)

The outer left and outer right screens are primary flight displays. They show:

- Airspeed in knots, with a moving tape.
- Barometric altitude in feet, with a moving tape.
- Heading, with a rotating compass.
- Ground speed in knots.
- Radar altitude (height above the ground) in feet.
- G load.
- A pitch and roll attitude display.
- Autopilot status: which seat has the autopilot engaged and whether altitude hold is active. See [Autopilot](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/autopilot/).
- Navigation: the autopilot mode, the active waypoint or approach runway, the localizer and glide slope scales, the drop countdown and a GEAR warning on final. See [Flight plan and approach](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/navigation/).

The cockpit also has a separate small attitude display.

## Engine page

The default page on the centre-left screen. For each of the four engines it shows:

- Power (HP), turbine temperature (MGT) and gas generator speed (NG), each as a needle gauge and a number.
- Propeller speed (NP) and fuel flow.
- Total fuel in kilograms, fuel percentage, and the endurance in minutes at the current throttle.

These gauges follow each engine's real speed. At idle an engine reads about 200 HP, 400 MGT and 62 % NG. At full power it reads about 4,600 HP, 870 MGT and 100 % NG. A stopped or failed engine reads zero. The power and fuel-flow figures are a guide only: actual fuel burn is described on the [Fuel](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/fuel/) page.

### Caution messages

The bottom of the Engine page lists caution messages. Each one shows only while its condition is true:

| Message | Shown when |
| --- | --- |
| RAMP OPEN | The cargo ramp is not closed. |
| CREW DOOR OPEN | The crew door is open. |
| GEAR DOWN | Airborne with the landing gear down. |
| FLIGHT ASSIST OFF | Flight assist is turned off. |
| APU RUNNING | The APU is running. |
| ENGINES OFF | No engine is running or starting. |
| ENG FIRE | An engine or the airframe is on fire, or the fire has spread to the wing tanks. |
| CARGO UNSECURED | Cargo in the bay is not tied down. |
| GEAR FAULT | A tyre has blown or the gear has collapsed. |
| FUEL LOW | Total fuel is at or below 15 %. |
| FUEL DUMP ON | Fuel is being dumped. |
| ENG FAILURE | One or more engines have failed. |
| MASTER CAUTION | Flashes for a fire, gear fault, low fuel or engine failure, and, when airborne, for unsecured cargo or a fuel imbalance. |

## Weight page

Shows the aircraft's weight and balance:

- Total weight, fuel weight and cargo weight in kilograms.
- How far the cargo has moved the centre of gravity, in centimetres, with a needle.
- Up to eight cargo items with their weight, each flagged UNSECURED if it is not tied down.

See [Loading vehicles](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/vehicles/) and [Cargo crates](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/crates/) for how loads affect the aircraft.

## Manifest page

A list of every cargo slot in the bay with its load name, weight, position and tie-down state (TIED, LOOSE or MOVING), plus gross weight, cargo weight, centre of gravity and slots used. A hint line at the bottom warns about unsecured loads or a centre of gravity out of limits. Full details are on [Loading vehicles](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/vehicles/) and [Cargo crates](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/crates/).

## Map page and release computer

The default page on the centre-right screen is a moving map with your aircraft symbol. Over the map, the release computer draws a crosshair where the next load released from the bay would land, with a status line, the distance to impact, a grid reference and a countdown. The **Release scale** action sets how the marker is scaled. Leave it on **AUTO** so the marker follows the map zoom by itself. Using the release computer for drops is covered in [Airdrop](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/airdrop/).

### Map zoom

**ZOOM** steps the map through six levels. Each press zooms in one step, and after the closest level it goes back to the widest. The label shows how many 100 m grid squares wide the map is, for example *ZOOM (31 tiles wide)*.

| Level | Width |
|---|---|
| 1 (widest) | about 61 squares (6 km) |
| 2 | about 31 squares (3 km) |
| 3 | about 15 squares |
| 4 | about 8 squares |
| 5 | about 5 squares |
| 6 (closest) | about 4 squares |

On a small terrain the widest levels stop where the whole map fits the screen. The release computer crosshair and the route follow the zoom by themselves when **Release scale** is on **AUTO**.

The Map page also draws the flight plan and the selected runway. See [Flight plan and approach](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/navigation/#the-route-on-the-map-page).

## NAV page

The last page on the centre-right screen lists the flight plan, the approach runway, the approach scales and the drop countdown. It is described in [Flight plan and approach](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/navigation/#the-nav-page).

## Radar page

A rotating-sweep display of aircraft around you, with range rings, contact altitudes and your heading. Use **Radar range** to change the range. See [Radar](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/radar/).

## Fuel page

A full picture of the fuel system:

- Four tank bars (LH OUTER, LH INNER, RH INNER, RH OUTER) with litres and percentage. A tank turns amber below 15 % and red below 5 %.
- Lateral balance: BALANCED, or LEFT HEAVY / RIGHT HEAVY with the difference in litres and a bar. The bar turns red at a 400 L imbalance.
- Total fuel in litres and endurance at the current throttle (amber below 45 minutes, red below 15 minutes).
- The current fuel transfer mode and the crossfeed valve position.
- One line per engine showing where it draws fuel from, or OFF / FAILED. With the crossfeed shut, an engine's line warns LOW below 10 % and DRY when its tank is empty.
- A hint line for the most urgent fuel problem, such as FUEL DUMP ACTIVE or a dry tank with the crossfeed shut.

How to use the fuel panel is explained on the [Fuel](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/fuel/) page.

## Systems page

Laid out like the real C-130J system status display:

- **Electrical**: voltage on each of the three phases and percentage load for the four engine generators and the APU generator. An offline generator reads OFF. Below that are the transformer-rectifier loads and the two DC bus voltages.
- **Hydraulics**: utility, booster and auxiliary system pressures, rudder boost pressures, and normal and emergency brake pressures.
- **Oil**: oil cooler flap position for each engine.
- **Fuel**: quantity in each tank and the total.
- **Oxygen**: the crew liquid oxygen quantity.

If the battery is off, the hint line reads BATTERY OFF - NO BUS POWER. What these numbers mean is explained on [Electrical and APU](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/electrical/), [Hydraulics](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/hydraulics/) and [Engines and oil](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/engines/).

## Checklist page

The Checklist page on the centre-left screen is a live checklist. It picks the right phase of flight by itself and ticks items off as you complete them, so you never need to page through it.

### Reading the checklist

The phase name is shown at the top and a hint line at the bottom tells you the most important next step. Each item has a mark:

| Mark | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `[X]` | Green | Done. |
| `[ ]` | Amber | Still to do, or in progress. |
| `[!]` | Red | Warning: something is wrong. |
| `-` | Grey | Information only. |

### How the phase is chosen

| Phase shown | When |
| --- | --- |
| BEFORE START | On the ground and not all four engines running. |
| BEFORE TAKEOFF | On the ground with all four engines running. |
| APPROACH / LANDING | Airborne with the gear down, or below 900 m (about 2,950 ft) above the ground. |
| CLIMB / CRUISE | Airborne, gear up and above 900 m above the ground. |

The aircraft counts as on the ground when its wheels are loaded or it is less than 15 m above the ground. Right after takeoff the checklist shows APPROACH / LANDING until you raise the gear and climb above 900 m.

### Fire items (any phase)

If any engine is burning, these rows appear at the top of the list in every phase:

| Item | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| ENGINE FIRE | Which engine is burning (red). |
| FIRE BOTTLES | Bottles left, for example 2/2, or EMPTY (red). |
| TANK FIRE . . . BOTTLE ONLY | The fire has reached the wing tanks, so only a fire bottle will put it out (red). |

The hint line then reads ENGINE FIRE - PULL FIRE HANDLE OR DISCHARGE BOTTLE, TANK FIRE - FUEL CUT WONT WORK, DISCHARGE BOTTLE, or NO AGENT LEFT - LAND OR SET DOWN NOW. See [Fire and damage](/reforger-c130-docs/systems/fire-and-damage/).

### BEFORE START

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| BATTERY . . . ON | The battery is on. |
| APU . . . RUNNING | The APU has finished starting. |
| CARGO . . . SECURED | No loose cargo. Red if anything is unsecured. |
| RAMP . . . CLOSED | The ramp is closed. |
| CREW DOOR . . . CLOSED | The crew door is closed. |
| FUEL . . . nn% | At least 25 % fuel. Amber at 15–25 %, red below 15 %. |
| ENGINE START . . . n/4 RUNNING | All four engines running. Reads IN PROGRESS during the start sequence. Red if some engines are running but the start is not complete. |

Hints, in order of priority: BATTERY FIRST - APU AND ENGINE START NEED BUS POWER, START APU BEFORE ENGINE SEQUENCE, ENGINE SEQUENCE RUNNING - STAND BY, READY FOR ENGINE START. See [Power-up and engine start](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/engine-start/).

### BEFORE TAKEOFF

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| ENGINES . . . n/4 RUNNING | All four engines running. |
| CARGO . . . SECURED | No loose cargo. |
| RAMP . . . CLOSED | The ramp is closed. |
| CREW DOOR . . . CLOSED | The crew door is closed. |
| FLAPS . . . SET | Flaps at any setting other than up. |
| TRIM . . . NEUTRAL | Trim close to neutral. |
| GEAR . . . DOWN | Gear down. |
| THRUST REVERSE . . . STOWED | Reverse thrust off. |
| FLIGHT ASSIST . . . ON/OFF | Information only. |
| GPWS . . . ON/OFF | The ground proximity warning system is on. |
| LIGHTS . . . | Landing and/or taxi lights on. Shows LANDING, TAXI, LANDING + TAXI or OFF. |
| FUEL . . . nn% | At least 25 % fuel. |

Extra red rows appear if they apply: FUEL DUMP . . . ACTIVE, GEAR . . . FAULT, ENGINE . . . FAILURE.

Hints, in order of priority: CLOSE RAMP AND DOOR BEFORE TAKEOFF, UNSECURED CARGO WILL SLIDE ON ROTATION, FLAPS SET FOR TAKEOFF, CLEARED FOR TAKEOFF. See [Taxi and takeoff](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/taxi-and-takeoff/) and [Trim and flaps](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/trim-and-flaps/).

### CLIMB / CRUISE

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| GEAR . . . UP | Gear up. |
| FLAPS . . . UP | Flaps up. |
| RAMP . . . CLOSED | The ramp is closed. |
| CARGO . . . SECURED | No loose cargo. |
| AUTOPILOT . . . ON/OFF | Information only. |
| GPWS . . . ON/OFF | GPWS on. |
| FUEL . . . nn% | At least 15 % fuel. |
| FUEL BALANCE . . . | BALANCED (under 25 L difference), L HEAVY / R HEAVY with litres, or the active transfer mode. Red at 400 L or more. Amber while a transfer mode is selected. |
| CROSSFEED . . . | OPEN (done), SHUT (amber), or SHUT - TANK LOW (red) when any tank is below 10 %. |
| ALTITUDE . . . nnnn FT | Information only. |

Extra red rows: FUEL DUMP . . . ACTIVE, ENGINE . . . FAILURE.

Hints, in order of priority: ENGINE OUT - TRIM AGAINST THE ASYMMETRY, FUEL IMBALANCE - SET FUEL PANEL TO AUTO BALANCE, GEAR STILL DOWN - DRAG AND FUEL BURN, FUEL LOW - PLAN THE DIVERSION.

### APPROACH / LANDING

| Item | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| GEAR . . . DOWN | Gear down. |
| FLAPS . . . SET | Flaps at any setting other than up. |
| GPWS . . . ON/OFF | GPWS on. |
| LIGHTS . . . | Landing and/or taxi lights on. |
| CARGO . . . SECURED | No loose cargo. |
| AUTOPILOT . . . ON/OFF | Autopilot off. Red while it is engaged. |
| RADAR ALT . . . nnnn FT AGL | Information only. |
| THRUST REVERSE . . . ARMED AFTER TOUCHDOWN | Reminder only. |

Extra red rows: GEAR . . . FAULT, ENGINE . . . FAILURE.

Hints, in order of priority: GEAR NOT DOWN, DISENGAGE AUTOPILOT BEFORE TOUCHDOWN, FLAPS FOR LANDING, STABLE - FLY IT DOWN. See [Approach and landing](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/landing/).

</Aside>
