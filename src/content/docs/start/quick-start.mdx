This page walks you through one short flight, from cold and dark to parked again, without explaining every system. Each step links to the page that covers it in depth.

You need a C-130J on or next to a runway. A Game Master can place one from the vehicle list. See [Game Master](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/game-master/).

## How the cockpit works

Most switches are **cockpit actions**: look at the control, scroll to pick the action and press <kbd>F</kbd>. Dials such as the throttle and trim are set by holding <kbd>F</kbd> and turning the mouse wheel. Flying keys (throttle, stick, gear, flaps) only work for the crew member who has the controls. See [Cockpit, seats and crew](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/cockpit-and-crew/).

## 1. Start up

<Steps>

1. Get in the **pilot** seat and use **Close Door**.
2. On the battery switch, use **Battery: ON**.
3. On the APU switch, use **APU: START** and wait 15 seconds, until the action reads **APU: STOP**.
4. On the engine start panel, use **Start engines**. The engines start one after another. All four are running after about 70 seconds.
5. Leave the APU running. It cannot be stopped while an engine runs.

</Steps>

Details: [Power-up and engine start](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/engine-start/).

## 2. Taxi

<Steps>

1. Release the parking brake: tap <kbd>Space</kbd>, or use **Parking brake: SET - release** in the cockpit.
2. Add a little throttle with <kbd>Shift</kbd>. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> reduces it. The throttle moves 50% per second while you hold the key and stays where you leave it.
3. Steer the nose wheel with <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd>, not <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd>. Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> to brake.
4. Line up on the runway centreline and stop.

</Steps>

Details: [Taxi and takeoff](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/taxi-and-takeoff/).

## 3. Take off

<Steps>

1. Lower the flaps with <kbd>F</kbd>. There is one flap setting, used for takeoff and landing.
2. Press <kbd>Home</kbd> to centre the trim.
3. Apply full throttle (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd>).
4. At about **190–200 km/h (around 105 kt)**, pull back gently with <kbd>S</kbd> and hold the nose 8–10° up.
5. Once you are climbing, raise the gear with <kbd>N</kbd>.
6. Raise the flaps with <kbd>F</kbd>.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Rotation and approach speeds are estimates based on the flight model's numbers. A heavily loaded aircraft needs more speed. Watch the aircraft, not just the number.
</Aside>

## 4. Fly a circuit

With flight assist on (it always is), the aircraft holds its pitch attitude when you let go of the stick. Use <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> to trim. Climb to a safe height, turn around, and fly back towards the runway.

If you want a break, the autopilot holds your current heading, altitude and speed. See [Autopilot](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/autopilot/).

## 5. Land

<Steps>

1. Line up with the runway from a few kilometres out.
2. Lower the gear with <kbd>N</kbd> and the flaps with <kbd>F</kbd>.
3. Fly the approach at about **210–230 km/h (115–125 kt)**, descending gently.
4. Touch down at about **185–200 km/h (100–110 kt)**. Keep the sink rate low: 4 m/s or more blows tyres, 7 m/s or more collapses the gear.
5. Pull the throttle to idle with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>, press <kbd>U</kbd> for reverse thrust, then add throttle to slow down. Reverse does nothing at idle.
6. Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> to brake. At walking pace, press <kbd>U</kbd> again to cancel reverse, or the aircraft starts rolling backwards.

</Steps>

Details: [Approach and landing](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/landing/).

## 6. Shut down

<Steps>

1. Stop, then tap <kbd>Space</kbd> to set the parking brake (it only sets below about 11 km/h).
2. Use **Shut down engines**.
3. Use **APU: STOP**, then **Battery: OFF**.
4. Get out.

</Steps>

Details: [Shutdown and parking](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/shutdown/).
