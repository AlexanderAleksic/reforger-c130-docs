This page covers what a Game Master can place from the mod and what to expect from those entities once they are in the world.

## What the mod adds

| Entity | Where to find it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **C130J Super Hercules** | Vehicles, US faction (airplane, modded content) | The standard aircraft. |
| **LC-130 (JATO)** | Vehicles, US faction, next to the C130J | The C-130J with eight JATO rocket bottles for short takeoffs. See [Aircraft variants](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/variants/). |
| **MC-130E Combat Talon I (Fulton STARS)** | Vehicles, US faction, next to the C130J | The C-130J with the Fulton STARS nose yoke for air pickups. See [Aircraft variants](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/variants/). |
| **M923A1 GBU-43/B Cargo** | Vehicles, US faction (truck, modded content) | An M923A1 truck with a GBU-43/B MOAB on its bed. |
| **Airdrop cargo box** | Systems | A storage pallet that can be filled with gear, moved by hand, loaded and airdropped. |

Use the content browser filters for the US faction or for modded content to find them quickly.

## Placing the aircraft

<Steps>

1. Open the Game Master editor and find **C130J Super Hercules** in the vehicle list.
2. Place it on flat, open ground. A runway or a large apron is best: the aircraft is big and needs room to taxi.
3. Close the editor and board through the crew door, or possess a character and walk to it.

</Steps>

A freshly placed C-130J is always **cold and dark**: battery off, APU off, engines off, ramp closed. The **parking brake is set**, so it stays where you put it even on a slope. The crew has to release it before the aircraft can roll (see [Taxi and takeoff](/reforger-c130-docs/basics/taxi-and-takeoff/)).

<Aside type="note">
The aircraft is flown by players only. The mod has no AI flight logic, so filling the pilot seat with an AI crew member does not make the aircraft fly.
</Aside>

## Placing the MOAB truck

The **M923A1 GBU-43/B Cargo** spawns ready to go, with the MOAB pallet already on the truck bed. Drive it to a C-130J and load the bomb as described in [GBU-43/B MOAB](/reforger-c130-docs/advanced/moab/). Placing it with crew puts an AI driver in the cab.

<Aside type="caution">
The MOAB has a 400 m danger radius. Keep this in mind when you place the truck and plan a drop near other players.
</Aside>

## Placing cargo boxes

Place **Airdrop cargo box** entries wherever you want resupply. Players fill them from an arsenal or their own inventory, push them into position and load them into the aircraft. See [Cargo crates](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/crates/).

Vehicles you place need nothing special to be loadable: any vehicle that fits the bay can be loaded. See [Loading vehicles](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/vehicles/).

## Behaviour with the editor open

- Opening the Game Master editor while you are using the cargo bay camera or a focused cockpit screen closes that view and returns you to the normal camera.
- The names you see in the editor are also used on the aircraft's displays. The release button and the cargo manifest show each load by that name (for example *RELEASE M151A2 Utility Truck*), and a vehicle dropped by parachute gets a map marker such as *CDS DROP - M151A2 Utility Truck*.

## Vehicle spawners

Outside of Game Master, the C-130J is in the US faction vehicle catalog with a large vehicle slot and a supply cost of 1200, so scenarios that build their vehicle lists from the faction catalog can offer it. The LC-130 and the MC-130E are in the same list with a supply cost of 1400 each.
