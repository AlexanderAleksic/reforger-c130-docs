This page covers dropping troops: the jump light, the jump positions, the jumpmaster's job, where to get parachutes, and the cockpit's view of the cargo bay.

## Seating in the cargo bay

| Position | How many | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passenger seats | 24, plus one bombardier position | Normal seating. Available at any time. |
| Jump positions | 24 | Standing positions for the stick. **Enter jump position** only appears while the jump light is **RED**. |
| Jumpmaster | 1 | At the rear of the bay. **Enter jumpmaster position**. Controls the jump lights and the ramp. |

## Parachutes

The C-130 carries **50 parachutes** in a **Parachutes** storage at the front of the cargo bay. Open it and put one on before the jump.

The **US Army paratrooper** character (available to Game Masters) already wears a parachute.

<Aside type="note">
The personal parachute, including how it opens and how you steer it, comes from the parachute mod the C-130 depends on, not from the C-130 itself. Use that mod's controls once you are out of the aircraft.
</Aside>

## Jump light

The jump light has three states:

| Light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Normal flight. Jump positions are locked. |
| **RED** | Stand up. The jump positions open and troops can take them. |
| **GREEN** | Go. The whole stick runs out of the ramp, then the light switches itself back off. |

The light is set by the **jumpmaster** from his position, or by the **pilot or co-pilot** from the cockpit. The actions offered depend on the current state:

- With the light off: **Jump light: RED**.
- With the light red: **Jump light: GREEN - GO** or **Jump light: OFF**.

**GREEN - GO** needs the ramp at **horizontal**. Until it is, the action shows *(set ramp horizontal first)*.

## The jump sequence

When the light goes green, the jump positions empty one after another, rearmost first, half a second apart. Each jumper runs down the bay and off the ramp edge on their own. The positions nearest the ramp are 5 m from the edge and the furthest are 16.5 m away, so jumpers leave about two-thirds of a second apart and a full stick of 24 is out in roughly 17 seconds.

The light returns to off by itself once everyone has gone. Each position checks the ramp as its jumper starts running, so if the ramp leaves horizontal mid-stick, the remaining jumpers stay aboard.

Jumping players are kept from flopping into a ragdoll for the first moment after they leave the aircraft.

## Procedure: paratrooper drop

<Steps>

1. **Before takeoff:** troops take a parachute from the **Parachutes** storage and sit in the passenger seats. A player takes the jumpmaster position, or the pilots run the drop from the cockpit.
2. **Run in:** fly toward the drop zone at a safe jump height, straight and level. The [Autopilot](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/autopilot/) helps here.
3. **Red light:** a few minutes out, set **Jump light: RED**. Troops leave their seats and use **Enter jump position**.
4. **Ramp:** set **Open ramp to horizontal (airdrop)**. It takes about 4 seconds. See [Cargo ramp](/reforger-c130-docs/cargo/ramp/).
5. **Green light:** over the drop zone, set **Jump light: GREEN - GO**. Hold the aircraft steady for the roughly 17 seconds the stick takes.
6. **Under canopy:** each jumper opens and steers their own parachute.
7. **Close up:** when the light goes off, close the ramp.

</Steps>

## Leaving in flight without the jump light

Passengers can also get out of the aircraft in flight with the normal get-out action, from a passenger seat, jump position or the jumpmaster position. You leave at your position's exit point and are protected from damage for 2 seconds. Make sure you are wearing a parachute first.

The pilot and co-pilot cannot bail out.

## Cargo bay camera

The pilot or co-pilot can watch the cargo bay from the cockpit with **View Cargo Bay**. This switches to a fixed camera inside the cargo bay, useful for watching loads roll out or the stick leave without a jumpmaster.

Press your interact key (<kbd>F</kbd> by default) to return to the normal view. The camera also closes if you open a menu, open the Game Master editor, or leave your seat. See [Cameras and views](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/cameras/) for the other views.

## For mission makers: test tools

The C-130 has two developer tools in the Workbench debug menu, under the C-130 category: **Spawn AI in jump spots**, which fills empty jump positions with AI paratroopers, and **Fire jump slide**, which starts the jump sequence (it can skip the ramp check for testing). Players do not see these in normal play.
