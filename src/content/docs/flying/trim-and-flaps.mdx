This page covers the pitch trim (manual and automatic) and the flaps.

## Pitch trim

Trim moves the elevator's neutral position, so the aircraft holds a pitch attitude without you holding the stick.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Range | ±30° of elevator (shown in degrees on the trim dial) |
| Manual trim speed | 3° per second while you hold the key |
| Spawn setting | 1.5° nose up |
| Neutral band for takeoff | within about ±4.5° |

### Adjusting trim

- **Keys**: hold <kbd>Page Up</kbd> to trim nose up and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> to trim nose down. Going from neutral to full trim takes 10 seconds.
- **Cockpit dial**: look at the trim wheel, hold the interaction key and scroll. The dial reads **Trim N°**. Each wheel step moves it 3°.
- **Reset**: press <kbd>Home</kbd> (gamepad: right stick click) or use **Reset trim** on the trim wheel. Trim returns to exactly neutral.

Only the crew member who has the controls can trim.

### Auto-trim

With flight assist on (the default), trim also works by itself:

- **Stick-force relief**: while you hold pitch input, trim slowly takes over that input. You need less and less stick to hold a climb or descent.
- **Attitude hold**: when you release the stick, the current pitch angle is captured and trim keeps the aircraft there. Climb at 8°, let go, and it keeps climbing at 8°.
- **Manual trim wins**: holding <kbd>Page Up</kbd>/<kbd>Page Down</kbd> overrides the hold. When you let go, the new attitude is captured.
- **Autopilot**: auto-trim pauses while the autopilot is engaged.

Because auto-trim keeps adjusting, the trim value drifts during flight. Before takeoff, press <kbd>Home</kbd> so the trim is back inside the neutral band. The checklist shows **TRIM NEUTRAL**, and the takeoff configuration warning checks it.

<Aside type="tip">
If the aircraft wants to pitch on its own after a long climb or descent, press <kbd>Home</kbd> to reset trim and re-trim from neutral.
</Aside>

## Flaps

The C-130 has a single flap setting: up or down.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Positions | Up / down |
| Deflection when down | 30° |
| Travel time | 2 seconds |
| Wait between moves | 2 seconds |

### Using the flaps

- **Key**: <kbd>F</kbd> (gamepad: D-pad right) toggles them.
- **Cockpit**: the flap lever offers **Lower flaps** / **Raise flaps**.

### What they do

- **More lift.** You can fly about 10 km/h slower before the stall. You lift off sooner and approach slower.
- **More drag.** The aircraft slows down faster and needs more power to hold speed.
- **Pitch change.** Expect a pitch change as they move. Flight assist and trim absorb most of it.

### When to use them

| Phase | Flaps |
|---|---|
| Takeoff | **Down** (the BEFORE TAKEOFF checklist asks for "FLAPS SET") |
| Climb and cruise | **Up** (the CLIMB / CRUISE checklist asks for "FLAPS UP") |
| Approach and landing | **Down** |

The mod does not set a maximum flap speed. Raise them once you are climbing safely after takeoff, and lower them as you slow down for the approach.
