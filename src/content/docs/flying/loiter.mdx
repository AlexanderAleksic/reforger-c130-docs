This page covers loiter mode, where the autopilot flies a circle around a point you pick on the map. It is useful for holding, overwatch and waiting for a drop clearance.

## Setting a loiter point

<Steps>

1. On your autopilot panel, use **Set loiter point (open map)**. The map opens.

2. Click the point you want to circle. The map closes and the autopilot engages in loiter mode around that point.

3. If the autopilot was off, it captures your current altitude and speed as its targets, as described in [Autopilot](/reforger-c130-docs/flying/autopilot/#engaging). If it was already on, it keeps the altitude and speed you had set.

</Steps>

If you close the map without clicking a point, nothing changes.

## How the orbit is flown

- **Direction**: chosen automatically. The aircraft joins the circle in whichever direction (left or right) needs the smaller turn from its current heading.
- **Joining**: the aircraft steers onto the circle and banks into the turn as it nears it, so it settles on the circle without weaving.
- **Bank**: the bank needed for the circle depends on your speed and the radius. It is limited to the autopilot's 60° maximum. A small radius at high speed needs a lot of bank. Slow down or use a larger radius.
- **Altitude and speed** are held exactly as in normal autopilot. You can still change them with **AP Altitude** and **AP Speed**.
- **Heading** is controlled by the orbit, so the **AP Heading** dial is hidden while you loiter.

## Changing the radius

While loitering, the seat that owns the autopilot sees **Loiter radius N m** on its panel. Hold the interaction key on it and scroll.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Default radius | 3,000 m |
| Range | 1,000-6,000 m |
| Step per wheel notch | 250 m |

The radius you set is kept for the next loiter.

## Turning loiter off

- **Turn off loiter** on the autopilot panel stops the orbit **and disengages the autopilot**. The action label shows the grid of the current loiter point.
- **Moving the stick** (pitch or roll input above 20%) also ends the orbit and disengages the autopilot.

<Aside type="caution">
If you use **Disengage autopilot** instead, the autopilot turns off but the loiter point is kept. **Turn off loiter** stays on the panel. Engaging the autopilot again returns you to the orbit. Use **Turn off loiter** when you want to clear the point.
</Aside>

After loiter ends, the throttle stays where the autothrottle left it. Take the controls and set your power.

## Moving the loiter point

**Set loiter point (open map)** is hidden while a loiter is active. To move the orbit, use **Turn off loiter**, then set a new point.
