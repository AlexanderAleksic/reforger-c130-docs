This manual covers everything you need to crew the C-130J Super Hercules in Arma Reforger, from your first engine start to airdropping vehicles and dropping a full stick of paratroopers.

## What the mod is

The C-130J is a four-engine turboprop transport. The mod models it as a working aircraft, not just a flying vehicle:

- **A real start-up.** Battery, APU and a timed engine start sequence. The aircraft spawns cold, with the parking brake set.
- **A strip-theory flight model** with weight, inertia, trim, flaps, flight assist, autopilot and an orbit (loiter) mode.
- **Aircraft systems** shown on cockpit displays: electrical, hydraulics, four engines with oil and failures, per-wing fuel tanks with transfer and dumping, and engine fires fought with fire handles and bottles.
- **Cargo operations.** Load vehicles and airdrop crates into a 12 m cargo bay, tie them down, and unload them on the ground. You can also release them in flight under parachutes or by LAPES extraction.
- **Paratroopers.** 24 jump positions, a jumpmaster, and a red/green jump light that sends the stick out the ramp.
- **Warning systems**: GPWS ground-proximity callouts, TCAS traffic advisories and a stall warning.
- **The GBU-43/B MOAB**, delivered out of the cargo bay through the same release system.

## How to read this manual

The manual is written to be read in order. Each section builds on the previous one:

1. **Start here.** Install the mod and fly a short first flight.
2. **Basics.** Seats, controls, start-up, takeoff, landing and shutdown.
3. **Flying.** How the aircraft handles, plus trim, flaps, autopilot, loiter and camera views.
4. **Aircraft systems.** The cockpit displays and each system in depth, with emergency procedures.
5. **Cargo operations.** Ramp, vehicles, crates, airdrop, LAPES and paratroopers.
6. **Advanced.** MOAB, radar, Game Master, multiplayer and troubleshooting.

If you only want to get airborne, read [Installation](/reforger-c130-docs/start/installation/) and then [Quick start](/reforger-c130-docs/start/quick-start/).
